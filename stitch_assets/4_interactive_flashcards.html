What is the primary electron acceptor in glycolysis, and what does it get reduced to?

Ref: Campbell Biology, Ch. 9.2Energy Harvest & Glycolytic Pathway
check_circleKey Solution & Mechanism

NAD⁺ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is the primary oxidizing agent. It is reduced to NADH.

lightbulb Quick Memorization Hook

Two molecules of NAD⁺ accept 2 electrons and 1 proton (H⁺) each, producing 2 NADH that shuttle chemical energy directly into the electron transport chain.
